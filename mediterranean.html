Mediterranean Cruises in a Suite

Sail between Barcelona, the Riviera, Rome, the Greek Isles and the Adriatic on small luxury ships, where every cabin is a suite and most have a private veranda. Fewer guests mean shorter queues in port, quieter decks and service that remembers your name.

When comparing voyages, look beyond the price: ports, time ashore, suite category and what the fare includes matter just as much. Your personal expert compares them for you and arranges business class flights and transfers to the ship.
